## 0.0.8 — 2026-10-02

### Added

- **createEncryptedLinkedProviderGrantStore wraps a grant store so access and refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-GCM at rest. Each ciphertext is bound to its grant, owner, provider and field. Saves always encrypt; reading older plaintext grants must be turned on explicitly with allowLegacyReads during a migration. The caller supplies and manages the key.**
